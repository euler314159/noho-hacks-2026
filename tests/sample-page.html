Volunteers restore the old riverside library

By Sam Rivera · 4 min read

After three years of fundraising, more than two hundred volunteers spent the weekend repairing shelves, painting walls and sorting donated books. The library first opened in 1912 and closed in 2019 when the roof began to leak.

ADVERTISEMENT

“It feels like the whole town showed up,” said the head librarian. Organisers say the reading room will reopen to the public next month, with a new section for large-print and audio books. Accessibility was a priority: the entrance now has a ramp, and every floor is reachable by lift. Local schools have already booked weekly visits, and a reading club for older residents will meet on Tuesday mornings.

What happens next

  • Electrical work finishes in two weeks.
  • The children’s corner opens in spring.
